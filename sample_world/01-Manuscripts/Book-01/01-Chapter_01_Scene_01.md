The frost gathered on the ramparts of the [[Citadel_of_Ash]] like spilled salt. Below in the caldera, the great geothermal hearth pulsed with a low, rhythmic heartbeat that vibrated through Raymond's boots.

Raymond adjusted his heavy mantle of wolfskin, his violet eyes narrowing as he gazed out over the jagged obsidian spires of the Ashmarch. The scouts had returned before dawn, their horses lathered and trembling. The vanguard of the [[Solar_Ascendancy]] was marching along the Sunstone Pass, banners of mirrored gold blazing in the morning light.

"They bring twelve battalions, my Lord," muttered Captain Bran, breath misting in the cold air.

Raymond reached down and touched the basalt parapet. With a focused breath, he drew upon the [[Investiture_of_Cinders]]. Heat flowed from the stone into his marrow, cooling the surrounding stone to a brittle ice while filling his veins with kinetic fire.

"Let them bring fifty," Raymond said softly. "The caldera yields to no emperor."
