The great doors of the Obsidian Throne Room swung inward with a groan of iron. [[Lady_Aurelia_Sol]] stepped onto the polished black floor, the hem of her sun-silk robes whispering against the flagstones.

She paused, taking in the cavernous hall. Above, soot-stained banners of [[House_Vael]] hung from vaulted ribs of basalt. At the dais sat Raymond Vael, his silver hair catching the amber glow of the hearth braziers.

"Lord Warden," Aurelia began, inclining her head just enough to satisfy diplomatic courtesy without ceding imperial dignity. Her golden eyes swept over the council of captains. "The Solar Ascendancy offers terms of eternal alliance. Grant us access to the lower ember-forges, and the Emperor will guarantee the sovereignty of the Ashmarch for three centuries."

Raymond leaned forward, resting his chin on his steepled fingers. "And if we refuse to surrender the ancient hearth of Mount Ignis?"

Aurelia smiled thinly. "The sun rises over all lands, Lord Vael. Even those buried under ash."
