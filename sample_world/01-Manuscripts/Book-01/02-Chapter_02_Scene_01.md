Dawn broke over Sunstone Pass in shards of crimson and fractured gold.

Raymond stood atop the narrow defile where the mountain road squeezed between two shearing cliffs of basalt. Below in the canyon, the imperial legion advanced in disciplined testudo formations, their mirrored shields deflecting the morning fog.

"Hold the line until they enter the funnel!" Raymond commanded.

He extended his hands, palms downward toward the volcanic fissure beneath his boots. The magma subterranean veins surged in response. Drawing deep on the [[Investiture_of_Cinders]], Raymond felt the bitter chill of thermodynamic transfer freeze the sweat upon his brow as raw volcanic heat coiled like a serpent within his chest.

With a sharp release, he drove the stored kinetic energy straight into the bedrock.

A deafening crack tore through the canyon. The forward ridge collapsed in an avalanche of shattered stone, cutting off the imperial vanguard from their supply train in a single stroke.
