# The Investiture of Cinders

## Core Mechanics & Channeling
Channelers (Ash-Keepers) absorb environmental thermal energy, cooling ambient air down to freezing temperatures while transmuting heat into dense kinetic force or white-hot thermal lances.

## Limitations & Costs
1. **Thermodynamic Rebound**: Overuse drains the channeler's core body temperature, causing rapid hypothermia and frostbitten extremities.
2. **Fuel Dependency**: Without a nearby combustion source or intense geothermal heat, kinetic output drops by 80%.
