# The Citadel of Ash

## Architectural Geography
Carved directly into the basalt caldera of Mount Ignis, the Citadel of Ash is a monumental subterranean stronghold built around the Primordial Hearth.

### Key Landmarks
- **The Obsidian Throne**: Carved from a single volcanic monolith.
- **The Lower Vaults**: Houses the conlang archives of the Old Empire.
- **The Outer Bastion**: 40-foot basalt walls reinforced with thermal runes.
