# Lord Raymond Vael

## Physical Dossier
- **Eyes**: Violet (illuminated faintly when channeling cinder investiture)
- **Hair**: Silver, cropped close to the scalp
- **Height**: 6'2"
- **Distinguishing Marks**: Burn scar running down the left forearm from the Siege of Valdane

## Psychological Profile & Core Motivation
Lord Raymond is the reluctant ruler of [[House_Vael]] following the Cataclysm. His primary motivation is safeguarding the remaining population of the [[Citadel_of_Ash]] from the encroaching solar ascendants. He adheres strictly to the ancient compacts of the Ashmarch.

## Magic Capabilities & Power Tier
- **Discipline**: [[Investiture_of_Cinders]]
- **Power Tier**: Tier 2 (Archmage / Battle-Adept)
- **Core Ability**: Pyric Kinetic Transmutation — converting thermal energy into kinetic shockwaves.
