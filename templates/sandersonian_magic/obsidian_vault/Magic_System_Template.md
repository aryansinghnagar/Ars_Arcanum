# Magic System: [Name of Magic System]

## 1. Sanderson's First Law: Foreshadowing & Mechanics
> *"An author's ability to solve problems with magic in a satisfying way is directly proportional to how well the reader understands said magic."*

- **Core Mechanism**: How does the magic work at a physical and metaphysical level?
- **User Agency**: What exact physical, verbal, or mental actions must an initiate perform?
- **Sensory Manifestation**: What does it smell like, look like, sound like when channeled?

## 2. Sanderson's Second Law: Limitations & Costs
> *"Limitations are greater than powers."*

- **Hard Constraints**: What can this magic *never* accomplish?
- **Resource Depletion**: What fuel is consumed (metals, emotional energy, blood, investiture)?
- **Biological / Mental Toll**: What are the physiological side effects of overuse?
- **Failure Modes & Vulnerabilities**: How can an opponent exploit or nullify this power?

## 3. Sanderson's Third Law: Depth Before Breadth
> *"Expand on what you have already, rather than adding something completely new."*

- **Ecological Impact**: How does this magic shape flora, fauna, and local weather?
- **Societal & Economic Integration**: How does it alter warfare, commerce, crime, and class structure?
- **Forbidden Mutations & Taboos**: What dangerous variations exist in secret?
