# Power Tier & Capability Scale

| Tier Level | Rank Designation | Channeling / Combat Capacity | Rarity in Population | Exemplar Individuals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Cosmic)** | World-Breaker | Can alter continental geography, shatter mountain ranges | 1 in 100,000,000 | The Ancient Primordials |
| **Tier 2 (Archmage)** | High Channeler | Can command weather over a kingdom, decimate legions | 1 in 1,000,000 | Lord Raymond Vael |
| **Tier 3 (Master)** | Battle-Adept | Sustained shields, targeted kinetic blast, rapid healing | 1 in 50,000 | Knight-Captains of the Order |
| **Tier 4 (Initiate)** | Disciple | Minor elemental manipulation, cantrips, spark lighting | 1 in 1,000 | Temple Acolytes |
| **Tier 0 (Mundane)** | Unawakened | Standard mortal physical capabilities | 99.9% | Common Populace |

## Channeling Burnout Thresholds
1. **Tier 1 Channelers**: High risk of spontaneous planar implosion if emotionally compromised.
2. **Tier 2-3 Channelers**: Symptoms of severance: sensory blindness, limb numbness, accelerated aging.
