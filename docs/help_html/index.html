Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Ars Arcanum, a dedicated, distraction-free Linux workstation built for speculative fiction authors and worldbuilders.

First 5 Minutes Workflow:
  1. Open The Forge (Welcome App) or press Super+Space to launch tools.
  2. Press Super+W to open your active Obsidian World Bible.
  3. Press Super+D or Super+N to start drafting scenes in FocusWriter or novelWriter.
  4. Press Super+F to enter Extreme Focus Mode (kiosk drafting with automated timer and Git auto-commit on exit).

The 6 Core Creative Arcs

1. Brainstorm & Lore

Store characters, locations, factions, and investiture rules with YAML frontmatter in Obsidian & Fantasia Archive.

2. Outline & Structure

Snowflake plotting, scene cards, and character psychology via novelWriter, Manuskript, and The Timeline Project.

3. Draft & Composition

Distraction-free fullscreen writing in FocusWriter with parchment backgrounds and Blanket ambient soundscapes.

4. Cartography & Visuals

Paint high-res continents in Krita with hardware OpenGL, and design vector heraldry crests in Inkscape.

5. Linguistics & Reference

Construct conlang dictionaries in PolyGlot, generate custom runic fonts in FontForge, and lookup words with GoldenDict.

6. Typesetting & Press

Compile 150,000 words in sub-250ms into 300 DPI print-ready PDFs using Typst and validated EPUB 3 via Pandoc.
