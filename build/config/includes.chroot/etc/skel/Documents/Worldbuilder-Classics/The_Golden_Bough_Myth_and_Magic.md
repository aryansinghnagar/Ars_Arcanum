# The Golden Bough: Principles of Magic & Sacred Kingship

## The Principles of Sympathetic Magic
If we analyze the principles of thought on which magic is based, they will probably be found to resolve themselves into two:

1. **The Law of Similarity (Homeopathic / Imitative Magic)**:
   - *Core Axiom*: Like produces like, or an effect resembles its cause.
   - *Worldbuilding Application*: From the rule of similarity, the magician infers that he can produce any effect he desires merely by imitating it. (e.g., pouring water onto hot stones to summon rainclouds; shaping a wax effigy of an enemy to cause phantom wounds).

2. **The Law of Contact or Contagion (Contagious Magic)**:
   - *Core Axiom*: Things which have once been in contact with each other continue to act on each other at a distance after the physical contact has been severed.
   - *Worldbuilding Application*: Whatever is done to a physical relic, severed hair, bloodstain, weapon, or footprint will inevitably affect the person or entity to whom the relic once belonged.

Both branches of magic may conveniently be comprehended under the general name of **Sympathetic Magic**, since both assume that things act on each other at a distance through a secret sympathy.

## The Magic of Sacred Kings & Divine Sovereignty
In many early societies, the king or chief is believed to be the dynamic center of the universe. From him flow the life, strength, and fertility of his people:
- If the king falls ill, the crops wither and herds fail to breed.
- If the king is slain or replaced, the seasonal cycle resets.
- Taboos surround the king to insulate the mortal realm from his raw divine charge (e.g., the king's feet must never touch bare soil, and the sun must never shine upon his crown when sleeping).
