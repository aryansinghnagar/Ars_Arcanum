# Bulfinch's Mythology: Mythic Archetypes & Monsters

## The Olympian Hierarchy
- **Zeus (Jupiter)**: Sovereign of gods and men, wielding the aegis and the thunderbolt. Represents patriarchal legal authority, storms, and the breaking of oaths.
- **Hera (Juno)**: Queen of heaven, guardian of marriages and dynastic legitimacy.
- **Athena (Minerva)**: Born fully armed from Zeus's brow. Goddess of wisdom, tactical statecraft, and defensive warfare (contrast with Ares's bloodlust).
- **Apollo (Phoebus)**: God of the sun, archery, music, and prophecy (the Pythian oracle at Delphi).

## Classical Beasts & Symbolic Anatomy
1. **The Chimæra**: A fire-breathing beast of divine origin, with the fore-parts of a lion, the body of a goat, and the tail of a serpent.
2. **The Sphinx**: A monster with the face and voice of a maiden, the body of a lion, and the wings of a bird. Plagued Thebes with riddles of mortal mortality.
3. **The Gorgons (Medusa)**: Beings with snaky tresses, brazen claws, and golden scales whose gaze petrifies mortal flesh into solid stone.
4. **The Pegasus**: The winged horse sprung from the neck of Medusa when struck by Perseus. Tamed by Bellerophon using the golden bridle of Athena.
