# The Prose Edda: Gylfaginning (The Beguiling of Gylfi)

## The Primordial Creation: Ginnungagap
In the beginning, there was neither sand nor sea nor cool waves. Earth was nowhere to be found, nor heaven above. There was **Ginnungagap** (the Yawning Void), and grass grew nowhere:
- To the South lay **Muspell**: luminous and hot, glowing and burning, impassable to outsiders. Surt sits at its border with a flaming sword.
- To the North lay **Niflheim**: dark and cold, whence flowed eleven venomous rivers (*Élivágar*).
- Where the rime-frost of Niflheim met the warm breath of Muspell, life stirred from the melting droplets, giving birth to **Ymir**, the primordial frost giant.

## Yggdrasil: The Cosmic Ash
The greatest and best of all trees is Yggdrasil. Its branches spread over the whole cosmos and stand above the heavens:
1. **Three Roots**:
   - First root reaches to the **Æsir** in heaven, beneath which lies the *Well of Urðr* (fate).
   - Second root reaches to the **Rime-Giants**, where *Mímir's Well* holds wisdom and intellect.
   - Third root reaches over **Niflheim**, where the dragon *Níðhöggr* gnaws the base.
2. **Cosmic Ecology**:
   - An eagle sits in the branches of the Ash, between whose eyes sits the hawk *Veðrfölnir*.
   - The squirrel *Ratatoskr* runs up and down the Ash, carrying insults between the eagle and the dragon.
   - Four harts stride across the branches and nibble the shoots.

## Ragnarök: The Twilight & Renewal of Worlds
The doom of the gods begins with **Fimbulwinter**: three continuous winters without summer, brother slaying brother for greed's sake. The sun is swallowed by Sköll; the moon by Hati. The stars fall from heaven. The Midgard Serpent surges ashore, vomiting venom over sea and sky.
Yet after the destruction, a second green earth emerges from the ocean. The fields bring forth unsown grain, and the survivors gather upon the plains of Iðavöllr.
